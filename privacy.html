Enzo Ai Short Maker

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

Who uses this app

Enzo Ai Short Maker is a personal tool used only by Daniel Julio Ochoa to manage his own YouTube channel, @DanielJulioOchoa. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it uses

With the channel owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to:

  • Read the channel's own video list: titles, descriptions, publish dates, view counts and lengths (scope youtube.readonly).
  • Upload new Shorts to the same channel (scope youtube.upload).

It does not read comments, subscribers, messages, analytics or any other account's data.

Where data is stored

Everything stays on the channel owner's own computer: the sign-in token, the video list and the clips the app makes. Nothing is sent to any server other than Google's own YouTube services.

Sharing

No data is sold, shared, or given to anyone else, and none is used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's folder removes all locally stored data.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the YouTube channel: youtube.com/@DanielJulioOchoa.
