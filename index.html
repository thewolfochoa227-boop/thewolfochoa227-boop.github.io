Enzo Ai Short Maker

A personal tool that turns full-length mariachi concerts, workshops and competitions into YouTube Shorts.

What it does

Enzo Ai Short Maker is used only by Daniel Julio Ochoa for the YouTube channel @DanielJulioOchoa. It:

  • reads the list of videos on that channel,
  • finds the most energetic musical moments in each video,
  • cuts them into vertical Shorts with a title banner, and
  • uploads them to the same channel as private videos for review.

It runs on the channel owner's own computer. It is not a public service, and nobody else can sign in to it.
